## Proposed bounded redesign

Keep CSP branding and the existing game/backend. Rebuild the game hierarchy around one active clue and an adjacent picker. Keep completed guesses below. Give mobile rankings a compact, readable row design. Simplify headers, account state, helper copy, and result actions; correct map proportions and selection access.

Use one Join leaderboard form: display name, optional school/organization, and email. Make the public fields explicit before submission; the Join action itself is the public opt-in. Email and individual guesses remain private. After confirmation, create the profile automatically and show success only after it is saved. A save failure offers retry without collecting the details again. Returning players use Sign in and retain their existing profile/privacy choice. Bind pending setup to the verified recipient and never apply another account's draft or overwrite an existing profile implicitly.

Recommend including today's guest game when joining, with that effect stated clearly on the initial form. Exclude archives and older guest results; retain conflict protections. This carryover choice is for the owner to confirm before implementation.

Success checks: first clue plus picker reachable together on the initial phone viewport; email confirmation is the last required joining action; readable rankings at 320px; account setup retries safely; existing profile/privacy and owner-bound scores remain intact. Add focused callback/reload/retry/account-switch tests and verify the changed UI in one bounded desktop/mobile capture batch, one fix batch, and at most one confirmation.
